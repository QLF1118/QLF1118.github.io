Lingfeng Qu
======
Lecturer, School of Cyberspace Security, Guangzhou University
Master’s Supervisor | Member of Prof. Zhihong Tian’s Research Team

I am currently a Lecturer at the School of Cyberspace Security, Guangzhou University, and a Master’s supervisor. I received my Ph.D. degree in Information Science and Technology from Southwest Jiaotong University in 2023 (combined M.Sc.–Ph.D. program). From 2021 to 2022, I was funded by the China Scholarship Council (CSC) to conduct joint research at the Christian Doppler Laboratory ATHENA, Alpen-Adria-University, Austria.

My research interests include reversible data hiding in the encrypted domain of multimedia (video, image, and 3D models), digital watermarking, and cryptanalysis. I teach the course Cryptographic Techniques and Applications.

During my doctoral studies, I led a Sichuan Province Science and Technology Innovation Project (Seedling Program) and received the Outstanding Ph.D. Dissertation Award in Cyberspace Security of Sichuan Province. To date, I have published over 30 papers in high-quality international and domestic journals and conferences, including IEEE TMM, IEEE TCSVT, ACM TOMM, IEEE IoTJ, Signal Processing, Journal of Information Security and Applications, Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, Journal of King Saud University – Computer and Information Sciences, Acta Electronica Sinica, and Journal of Computer Research and Development, among which 19 papers are as the first or corresponding author.
